My Priorities

7 core issues I've championed — and will keep fighting for — on Fairfax City Council.

Nonpartisan

These Aren't Democrat or Republican Issues

These are Fairfax issues — the things that make our city work for everyone. I govern to solve problems, not to score political points.

1. Public Safety

Fully funded police & fire with community-oriented policing that builds trust between officers and residents.

I'll continue: Advocate for competitive first responder compensation and keep emergency response times among the best in the region.

2. Fiscal Responsibility

As a CPA, every number is my responsibility. Under my tenure, Fairfax achieved an AA bond rating and an 8.5% tax rate reduction.

I'll continue: Every dollar tracked, every expense justified. Efficient government that delivers results.

3. Environment & Parks

Fairfax's tree canopy and green spaces are what make our city special. I've championed park expansions and environmental protections.

I'll continue: Push for sustainable development, increase green infrastructure, and protect our parks.

4. Transparency

Government works for you — and you have a right to know what it's doing. I've championed open meetings, accessible budgets, and clear communication.

I'll continue: Online budget tracking tools, regular town halls, public access to all Council decisions.

5. Strong Schools

Great schools are the foundation of a thriving city. Consistent advocate for adequate funding, safe facilities, and programs that set kids up for success.

I'll continue: Maintain close partnership with the school board and support teacher retention and modern facilities.

6. Infrastructure

Potholes, aging water mains, failing traffic signals — not glamorous, but they matter. I've prioritized infrastructure maintenance and modernization.

I'll continue: Capital improvement program, state funding advocacy, and keeping our streets and utilities in good repair.

7. Community & Engagement

A strong city is built by engaged residents. I've worked to lower barriers to civic participation and expand public comment opportunities.

I'll continue: Regular town halls, expanded community events, and ensuring every resident has a voice at City Hall.

"It takes courage to ask questions. It takes even more courage to stand up for what you believe in, even when it isn't the popular opinion."

— Stacy R. Hall
