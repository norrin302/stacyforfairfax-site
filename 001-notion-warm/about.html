About Stacy

Finance professional. Community advocate. Public servant. Here's my story.

A Lifetime in Fairfax

I'm a Certified Public Accountant and former small business owner. I raised two kids in Fairfax and have dedicated nearly two decades to public service on City Council.

Before Council, I served on the Fairfax Planning Commission for 3 years, giving me a foundation in land use, development, and the long-term vision our city needs.

Career Timeline

Fairfax Planning Commission

Three years shaping land use and development policy before joining City Council.

2018 – Present

Vice Mayor of Fairfax

Leading Council meetings and representing Fairfax at regional and state levels for over 5 years.
