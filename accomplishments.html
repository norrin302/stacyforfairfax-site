Governance Approach

Beyond Individual Votes or Projects

My approach has centered on making decisions based on facts, protecting our residents, taxpayers and their wallets, and ensuring long-ago planned projects still serve the needs of our residents today.

  • Asking tough questions — It takes courage to ask questions. Raising concerns is part of my responsibility to represent our residents.
  • Fiscal accountability — Ensuring residents understand the budget, tax rates, and the challenges we face as a city.
  • Transparent decision-making — Building trust through open and honest communication about city business.
  • Future-focused planning — Making sure projects serve today's residents, not just abstract future visions.

Fiscal Responsibility & Taxpayer Protection

Budget Transparency

A major focus has been ensuring residents understand the budget, the tax rates and the struggles we face as a city. Knowledge is the foundation of good governance.

Tax Pressure Analysis

Improved transparency around the true drivers of local tax pressure and reinforced accountability in financial decision-making.

Needs vs. Wants

Identifying needs vs. wants and ensuring our needs are being met before spending on our wants. Every dollar belongs to taxpayers.

Resource Efficiency

Making sound financial decisions that support sustainable growth and stability for current and future generations.

Strong City Schools

Advocating for Our Students

I recognize the importance of a strong public school system and I support improvements to our schools so our students have safe, secure, modern and innovative learning spaces. Our students are the future, and they deserve the best we can provide.

  • Served as City Council's liaison to the School Board, ensuring city resources support our educational institutions
  • Proud that my two kids attended Providence Elementary School, Katherine Johnson Middle School, and are currently at Fairfax High School
  • Advocated for an educational feasibility study to assess our current agreement with FCPS
  • Support understanding what it might cost to run our own school system — it's not possible to make data-driven decisions without data

Environment & Responsible Development

Green Space Protection

Protecting and maintaining our beautiful parks and green spaces for our residents now, and for generations to come. We must preserve the few forests we have left.

Sustainable Practices

Working together to promote renewable energy, clean energy and green buildings. Supporting the Climate and Energy Manager role in the FY2027 budget.

Leaf Blower Initiative

Proposed common-sense solutions like restricting gas-powered leaf blower hours and supporting rebates on electric alternatives — similar to what Vienna and DC have implemented.

Responsible Development

City decisions around land use require careful balance between growth and preservation. Development should be responsible, not rushed.

How I Approach Council Service

Ask Questions

It takes courage to ask questions. Asking questions and raising concerns is not opposition — it's responsible representation.

Listen First

Outreach to communities, especially those most impacted by projects. Understanding how residents think and feel.

Explore Options

Consider both pros and cons. Look for alternate solutions that might better serve our residents.
