My Priorities

I love our city — these 6.2 square miles and ~24,000 people are our home. I want to keep our charm and small town feel, support our local businesses, and preserve our green spaces and waterways.

Why I'm Nonpartisan

I think many of the concerns our residents have do not fall along party lines, and do not require a Republican or a Democrat to make a decision. Rather, it takes a council dedicated to open dialogue, communication and putting the needs of our current and future residents first.

It Takes Courage to Ask Questions

Asking questions and raising concerns is not an indication of opposition to a project — it's part of my responsibility to ensure I'm representing our residents and making the most informed decision possible on their behalf.

Regardless of your political affiliation, I ask that your vote is made in the best interest of our city, and not be based on politics that live outside these 6.2 miles. As a City Council member, I know I work for YOU, a city resident, who has chosen to call our beautiful city home.

Public Safety & Security

We need to ensure our neighborhoods, businesses, parks, paths and downtown areas are welcoming, and residents feel safe at all times. Every resident, old, young or anywhere in between, deserves to feel safe.

  • Address residents' concerns about rising crime with additional resources
  • Ensure our police department is fully staffed and capable
  • Work to help those experiencing homelessness with access to shelters, financial assistance and housing programs
  • Support comprehensive services including case management, job training, and mental health services
  • Promote affordable housing initiatives and rent subsidies

Environment & Green Spaces

We must protect and maintain our beautiful parks and other green spaces for our residents now, and for generations to come. There are many options to add bike/pedestrian paths both in and around our city, without destroying our forests and trees.

  • Preserve our forests — we must protect the few forests we have left
  • Promote use of renewable energy, clean energy and green buildings
  • Support the Climate and Energy Manager role in FY2027 budget
  • Restrict hours for gas-powered leaf blowers and propose bans on Sundays/federal holidays
  • Provide rebates on electric leaf blowers and lawn equipment for residents and landscaping companies
  • Move toward cleaner, quieter neighborhoods by recognizing that gas-powered lawn equipment can be loud and harmful, and encouraging residents and landscaping companies to consider electric alternatives
  • Ensure areas are well-lit and well maintained for walking, biking, and scootering

Fiscal Responsibility

Fiscal responsibility is about making sound financial decisions that support sustainable growth and stability, ensuring resources are used efficiently and effectively for the benefit of current and future generations.

  • Needs vs. wants should be identified, and decisions made to ensure needs are met first
  • Build trust with the public through open and transparent financial conversations
  • Support strong City Manager leadership to restore trust
  • Ensure residents understand the budget, tax rates and struggles we face
  • Make data-driven decisions with proper research and analysis

Transparency & Community Engagement

Each project should consider our existing residents, yet plan for common sense growth. I have continued to advocate for greater transparency and more open communication between our city's leaders and residents.

  • City Council leaders should represent the voice of our residents
  • Require outreach to communities, especially those most impacted by projects
  • Hold conversations and open houses to ensure we understand all residents
  • Consider potential impact to existing infrastructure
  • Explore both pros and cons for residents before making decisions
  • Ensure projects serve the needs of residents today, not just abstract future goals

Strong City Schools

I recognize the importance of a strong public school system and I support improvements to our schools so our students have safe, secure, modern and innovative learning spaces. Our students are the future.

  • Support improvements to our schools for safe and modern learning spaces
  • Proud that my kids attended Providence Elementary, Katherine Johnson Middle School, and Fairfax High School
  • Advocated for an educational feasibility study to assess our FCPS agreement
  • Support knowing what it might cost to run our own school system
  • Make data-driven decisions about pros and cons for students, families, and taxpayers

Housing & Affordability

Working to ensure Fairfax remains accessible to families at all stages of life. Cities with robust affordable housing initiatives tend to have higher success rates in helping individuals secure stable housing.

  • Balance growth with preserving our community character
  • Ensure housing options exist for teachers, first responders, and essential workers
  • Support affordable housing initiatives and rent subsidies
  • Work with housing programs for those in need
  • Help those experiencing homelessness or close to it with comprehensive support services

Walkability & Connectivity

Whether you are walking your dog, riding your bike, or renting one of the many scooters and bikes in the city, areas should be well-lit and well maintained for everyone to enjoy.

  • Add bike/pedestrian paths in and around our city
  • Ensure areas are well-lit and well maintained
  • Support alternative transportation options including scooters and bikes
  • Connect neighborhoods to parks, downtown, and schools
  • Make Fairfax accessible to everyone, regardless of age or ability
