About Stacy

Meet the Candidate Who's Fighting for Fairfax

As the daughter of small business owners in Wilmington, DE, I grew up in a bagel and Italian bread bakery. My mom was a public high school teacher, and I remember many nights spent together watching her grade tests and papers on the couch. I learned the value of hard work at a very early age.

I graduated from the University of Delaware — go Blue Hens!! — with a Bachelors of Science in Finance and minors in Economics and Biology. I played field hockey and lacrosse in middle and high school, and t-ball and softball through elementary and middle school.

I relocated to the DC metro area in 2001 and have lived in Fairfax since 2011 with my husband and two sons and our two dogs. When we moved here, I was pregnant with our second child and our oldest was almost 2. They have been so fortunate to grow up in our community, attend our city schools, and have many of the same friends now that they made in preschool and kindergarten. Fairfax City truly is a gem, and I want to help protect, maintain and enhance what makes us shine.

What I Stand For

My Priorities for Our Community

I love our city — these 6.2 square miles and ~24,000 people are our home. I want to keep our charm and small town feel, support our local businesses, and preserve our green spaces and waterways.

Public Safety & Security

Every resident, old, young or anywhere in between, deserves to feel safe. We need to address residents' concerns about rising crime by providing additional resources to our police and public safety officers, ensuring our police department is fully staffed and capable of protecting the community.

Environment & Green Spaces

We must protect and maintain our beautiful parks and other green spaces for our residents now, and for generations to come. We must preserve the few forests we have left and work together to promote renewable energy, clean energy and green buildings.

Fiscal Responsibility

Fiscal responsibility is about making sound financial decisions that support sustainable growth and stability. Needs vs. wants should be identified, and decisions should be made to ensure our needs are being met before we spend money on our wants.

Transparency & Engagement

Each project should consider our existing residents, yet plan for common sense growth. I have continued to advocate for greater transparency and more open communication between our city's leaders and residents. Our City Council leaders should represent the voice of our residents.

Strong City Schools

I recognize the importance of a strong public school system and support improvements to our schools so our students have safe, secure, modern and innovative learning spaces. Our students are the future, and they deserve the best we can provide.

Housing & Affordability

Working to ensure Fairfax remains accessible to families at all stages of life. We must balance growth with preserving our community character and ensure that housing options exist for our teachers, first responders, and essential workers.

Walkability & Connectivity

Whether you are walking your dog, riding your bike, or renting one of the many scooters and bikes in the city, areas should be well-lit and well maintained. There are many options to add bike/pedestrian paths both in and around our city.

Small Business Support

Our local businesses are the heart of our community. I am committed to supporting our small business owners, attracting new businesses to our city, and ensuring Fairfax remains a vibrant place to shop, dine, and gather.

Nonpartisan Leadership

Many of the concerns our residents have do not fall along party lines. It takes a council dedicated to open dialogue, communication and putting the needs of our current and future residents first. Regardless of your political affiliation, I ask that your vote is made in the best interest of our city.

Track Record of Results

Responsible Stewardship. Stronger Future.

Over the past years on City Council, my focus has been simple: make decisions based on facts, protect our residents, taxpayers and their wallets, and ensure planned projects serve the needs of our residents today.

Fiscal Transparency

Improved transparency around the true drivers of local tax pressure and reinforced accountability in financial decision-making. Ensured residents understand the budget, tax rates and the struggles we face as a city.

Capital Improvement Program

Advocated for comprehensive oversight of aging infrastructure requiring thoughtful reinvestment — from public buildings to essential infrastructure systems we rely on every day.

School Board Liaison

Served as City Council's liaison to the School Board, advocating for our students' needs and ensuring city resources support our educational institutions effectively.

Environmental Stewardship

Protected green spaces and advocated for sustainable development practices. Proposed common-sense solutions like restricting gas-powered leaf blower use and promoting electric alternatives.

Housing Affordability

Working to help those experiencing homelessness or close to it with access to resources such as shelters, financial assistance, housing programs, and comprehensive support services.

Asking Tough Questions

It takes courage to ask questions. Asking questions and raising concerns is not opposition to a project — it's part of my responsibility to ensure I'm representing our residents and making the most informed decision possible on their behalf.

In the Community

Fairfax is My Home

I am the Controller for a company that advances care and innovation in the animal health sector. Prior to that, I was a public accountant for 9 years. I also run my own small business in the city, providing accounting, bookkeeping and tax services.

I love cheering on our local sports teams, especially our FHS football and basketball teams on which my kids play. You can often find us at Rock The Block or many of the other amazing, family-friendly events our city hosts!
